# CV


Evolutionary botanist, interested in the diversity and evolution of tropical rain forest plants, in particular *Annonaceae* and *Connaraceae*, but easily fascinated by other plant diversity and beyond. 

Currently based at the [Sukkulenten-Sammlung](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/sukkulentensammlung/en.html) in Zürich (Switzerland). 


## Education

2022 - 2025: PhD Biology and Evolutionary Ecology – IRD Montpellier /  University of Montpellier (France)

2020 - 2022: MSc Plant Science – University of Basel (Switzerland)

2017 - 2020: BSc Biology, Major in Animal and Plant Sciences - University of Basel (Switzerland)
